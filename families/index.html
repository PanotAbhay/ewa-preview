For families and carers

You may care for someone who could enjoy a creative group. Here is what the group is like, and how to help them take the first step.

What the group is like

The group meets every week to make art together. It is relaxed and friendly. People do not need art experience, and nobody has to talk about their health.

Members support each other. The group is for adults Age limit.

How you can help

  • Show them this website, or the Join a group page.
  • Offer to send a message with them, or for them.
  • Ask us if you can come with them the first time. Policy on companions

Ask first

Please talk to the person before you contact us about them. We will only contact someone who knows about it and agrees.

What EwA can and cannot do

EwA is an art group with peer support. It is not a health service.

  • It does not give therapy or medical care.
  • It cannot help in a crisis. If someone is in danger, call 999.
  • It cannot tell you about another adult's attendance without their agreement.

Send a message

Need help now?

EwA is not a crisis service. If you or someone else is in danger, call 999. For urgent mental health help in England, call NHS 111 and choose option 2. You can talk to Samaritans free at any time on 116 123.
