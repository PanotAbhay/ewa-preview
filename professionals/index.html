Joining

What the group is not

EwA is not a clinical service. It does not give art therapy. It does not give diagnosis, treatment, care plans or crisis support. People stay with their usual health and care services.

Referring someone

  1. Talk to the person. Check that they want to come and agree to you contacting us.
  2. Send a message. Give the person's first name and the best way to reach them, or ask us to contact you.
  3. We get in touch. We invite the person to a group.

Please do not send medical records, a diagnosis or other health details. If the person wants to share something with us, they can do that themselves.

Need help now?

EwA is not a crisis service. If you or someone else is in danger, call 999. For urgent mental health help in England, call NHS 111 and choose option 2. You can talk to Samaritans free at any time on 116 123.
