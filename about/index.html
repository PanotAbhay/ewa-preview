About us

EwA began in 2013 as a weekly art group in a church room on the Isle of Dogs. It was started by an artist who finds that art says what words cannot.

From 2013 to today

  1. 2013Phillip Singh starts a self-help art group for people who have experienced mental health issues. It meets every Wednesday evening on the Isle of Dogs, with funds from NHS East London. Wording of funding
  2. 2014Members, residents and professional artists paint a mural called “HOPE” for a women's hostel in East London. Members show their work at an NHS event.
  3. 2015The group writes down its aims: to express feelings through art, to build confidence and self-esteem, and to support each other. Members lead the projects and make decisions together.
  4. 2020A second website introduces the group as “Expressions: Well-being through Art”.
  5. 2023Art sessions continue every Wednesday, with food and drinks.
  6. 2026EwA plans its next step: a clear approach, from recovery to creative work.

Each year comes from EwA's two websites and a 2013 NHS press release. Add events, such as exhibitions, that are missing.

Phillip Singh, founder

Phillip is an artist from Tower Hamlets. He started the group so that other people could express themselves through art, as he does.

“I find art is my language more than words.”

Phillip Singh, 2013

How the group works

  • Members lead the group and make decisions together.
  • Members support each other.
  • Everyone is welcome to make art, whatever their skill.

Need help now?

EwA is not a crisis service. If you or someone else is in danger, call 999. For urgent mental health help in England, call NHS 111 and choose option 2. You can talk to Samaritans free at any time on 116 123.
