Work with us

For businesses, venues, creative organisations and community groups. Commission a mural or artwork, host an exhibition, or offer creative work.

  • Commission a mural or artwork

    EwA members can design and paint murals and other artwork. You tell us what you need, and we work from your brief.

    In 2014, members, residents and professional artists painted the “HOPE” mural for a women's hostel in East London.

    Prices: How commissions are priced

  • Host an exhibition

    Offer a wall, a window or a room. An exhibition lets members show their work to new people, and can lead to sales.

Need help now?

EwA is not a crisis service. If you or someone else is in danger, call 999. For urgent mental health help in England, call NHS 111 and choose option 2. You can talk to Samaritans free at any time on 116 123.
