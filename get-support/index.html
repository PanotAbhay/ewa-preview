Join a group

You do not need art experience. You do not need to talk about your health. Come and make something with other people.

How to join

  1. Send a message. Use the form on this page. Give your name and how we should reply.
  2. We get in touch. We tell you when and where the next group meets, and answer your questions.
  3. Come along. Come when it suits you. You can watch first, or start straight away.

Confirm the joining steps. Does a new member meet someone first? Is a referral ever needed? Is there a limit on numbers?

Who the groups are for

The groups are for adults who have experienced, or are experiencing, mental health difficulties. They also welcome people with experiences such as ADHD, dyslexia, surviving domestic abuse, and PTSD or other trauma.

If you are not sure the group is right for you, send us a message and ask. You do not have to tell us why you want to come.

Age limits, area limits and the full list of who the groups serve are not yet confirmed. The 2013 funding was for people who had experienced mental health issues.

Questions people ask

I have never painted. Can I come?

Yes. Many people start with no experience. The group is about expressing yourself, not about being good at art.

Will I have to talk about my mental health?

No. You can talk if you want to, or just make art. Members support each other in their own way.

Can I bring someone with me?

Ask us in your message. Policy on companions and support workers

Is this art therapy?

No. EwA is a peer support art group. It does not give therapy or medical treatment. Members help each other, and nobody needs to be an artist.

Need help now?

EwA is not a crisis service. If you or someone else is in danger, call 999. For urgent mental health help in England, call NHS 111 and choose option 2. You can talk to Samaritans free at any time on 116 123.
