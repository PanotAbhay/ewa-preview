What we do

The weekly art group is at the centre. Around it, members show their work, paint murals and take part in creative projects.

    Paint, draw and make art with other people, in a relaxed group. Materials are there for you. There is food and something to drink.

Where the artwork comes from

Some members paint every week. Others come for a while and then move on. The artwork on this site comes from EwA's own websites.

Collect new artwork with each artist's written permission. Agree how each artist wants to be named.

Need help now?

EwA is not a crisis service. If you or someone else is in danger, call 999. For urgent mental health help in England, call NHS 111 and choose option 2. You can talk to Samaritans free at any time on 116 123.
