Privacy

We collect as little information as we can. We use it only to reply to you.

This is a draft notice. A person who knows UK data protection law should check it before the site goes live.

This preview

This preview does not send, store or share anything you type in its forms. It does not use cookies, analytics or tracking.

If you hide the review notes, your browser remembers that choice on your own device. We cannot see it.

When the website is live

What we collect

When you send a message, we collect your name, how to reply to you (email or phone), what your message is about and your message. If you tell us, we also collect your organisation's name.

We do not ask for health details or a diagnosis. Please do not put them in your message.

Why we use it

We use your details to reply to you and, if you want, to invite you to a group. We do not use them for marketing. We do not sell them or share them, unless the law says we must, or someone is at risk of serious harm.

Our lawful basis is legitimate interests To be checked.

How long we keep it

We keep messages for a time to agree, for example 12 months, then delete them.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your information. You can ask us to correct it or delete it. To do this, contact us.

If you are not happy with how we use your information, tell us first. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

Need help now?

EwA is not a crisis service. If you or someone else is in danger, call 999. For urgent mental health help in England, call NHS 111 and choose option 2. You can talk to Samaritans free at any time on 116 123.
